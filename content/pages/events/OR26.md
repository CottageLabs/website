We are proud to be [sponsoring](https://or2026.openrepositories.org/sponsors/supporters-2026/) the Open Repositories Conference 2026, a virtual event bringing the community together to explore open infrastructure, repositories, and scholarly systems.

## Our presentations

* <a href="https://zenodo.org/records/20788963" title="Slides for 'Public Health Data in Context: Publishing the RKI research graph' presentation">**Public Health Data in Context: Publishing the RKI research graph**</a>

    _Richard Jones_

    Presenting with the Robert Koch Institute, we explore how InvenioRDM supports a public health research graph, linking complex data from projects to datasets.

* <a href="https://zenodo.org/records/20789098" title="Slides for 'Ask a robot: adding AI search to a repository'">**Ask a robot: adding AI search to a repository**</a>

    _Steve Eardley_

    We take a practical look at AI-powered search in repositories, moving beyond keywords to more meaningful discovery.

* <a href="https://zenodo.org/records/20788951" title="Slides for 'Beyond FAIR: Designing Cognitive Accessibility in Data-Rich Repositories'">**Beyond FAIR: Designing Cognitive Accessibility in Data-Rich Repositories**</a>

    _Aga Domańska_

    Explore how better UX can reduce cognitive load and make repository systems easier to use in practice.
